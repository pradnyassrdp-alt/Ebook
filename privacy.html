Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information I collect when you visit 10-minuteenergyreset.in or buy The 10-Minute Energy Reset, how I use it, and the choices you have. I keep it simple: I collect only what I need, and I never sell your details.

Who I am

This website and ebook are run by Pradnya Wagh, a breathwork and meditation teacher based in India. You can reach me at 10minutesenergyresetebook@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • When you buy: your name, email address, any other details the checkout asks for, and your payment reference.
  • When you contact me: your messages and the details you share in them, by email or Instagram direct message.
  • When you visit this website: information about your visit, such as the pages you view, the buttons you click, your device and browser type, and your approximate location from your IP address. This is collected through cookies, the Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity.

Payments

Payments are processed by Superprofile and its payment partners. I never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

How I use your information

  • To deliver your ebook by email, and to send it again if it does not arrive.
  • To answer your questions and support your practice, including the 30-Day Shift Promise.
  • To send emails related to your purchase and practice. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand how visitors use this website and to measure and improve my advertising on Facebook and Instagram.
  • To keep records required by law, such as tax and payment records.

Cookies, the Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta Platforms. It places cookies in your browser and tells Meta when you visit a page, start checkout or complete a purchase. This helps me show my ads to people who may find them useful and measure whether they work. Meta's use of this information is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

This website also uses Microsoft Clarity, a free tool from Microsoft that shows how visitors use the page: where they click, how far they scroll, and anonymised recordings of visits. It helps me make the website clearer and easier to use. Clarity hides text you type by default, and I do not use it to identify you. Microsoft’s use of this information is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage how Meta uses your activity for ads in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences.

Who I share it with

I never sell or rent your personal information. I share it only with:

  • Superprofile and its payment partners, to process your order and deliver your ebook.
  • The email service I use to send your ebook and practice emails.
  • Meta, through the Pixel, as described above.
  • Microsoft, through Clarity, as described above.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Reviews

If you send me a review, I share it publicly only with your permission, and only with your first name and city.

How long I keep it

I keep your details only for as long as I need them to deliver your ebook, support your practice and meet legal and tax requirements. After that, I delete them.

Keeping it safe

I use trusted services with their own security measures and limit access to your details to what is needed. No method of storing or sending information online is completely secure, but I take reasonable care to protect it.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or to stop sending you emails. Write to 10minutesenergyresetebook@gmail.com and I will respond as soon as I can.

Age

The ebook is intended for adults aged 18 and above. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Other websites

This website links to Superprofile, Instagram and Facebook. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use them.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
